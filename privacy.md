# Car Key — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-04-24_

Car Key ("the app") is a companion application for the Chery Jetour G700
vehicle. It runs on iPhone and Apple Watch and communicates directly with
the vehicle's head unit over Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE). This policy
describes what information the app handles and how.

## Information the app does not collect

The app does **not** collect, transmit, store on any server, or share any
of the following:

- Personal information (name, email, phone number)
- Location data
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Crash reports
- Advertising identifiers or any other tracking identifiers

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

## Information stored locally on your device

To function, the app stores the following on your iPhone / Apple Watch
only. This data never leaves the device:

- The Bluetooth identifier of the paired vehicle head unit
- A pairing secret generated during the initial pairing handshake, used
  to authenticate commands to the vehicle
- User preferences (e.g., last-used climate settings)

Uninstalling the app removes all of this data.

## Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth Low Energy to communicate directly with the
vehicle's head unit. All communication stays on the local Bluetooth link
between your device and the vehicle. No data is sent to the developer, to
Chery / Jetour, or to any third party.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly
collect data from anyone.
